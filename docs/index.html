Beyond Class Imbalance:
Fisher Information Diagnostic for Imbalance Remedy Selection

Mabrouka Salmi  ·  Dalia Atif  ·  Sebastian Ventura

National High School of Statistics and Applied Economics, Algeria  ·  University of Córdoba, Spain

This page provides supplementary materials for the paper introducing the Fisher Information Imbalance Signature (FIIS) — a principled diagnostic framework that decomposes the Fisher information ratio R = Rn · Rp · Rg · C into interpretable components, enabling hypothesis-driven remedy selection for class imbalance. Materials include full experimental results, per-dataset FIIS characterisations, per-classifier analyses, and a medical diagnosis case study.

Real Data Characterisation & Archetype Discovery

FIIS components (Rn, Rp, Rg, C, R) computed from logistic regression on the original training data across 30 cross-validation folds. Archetype assigned per fold based on the dominant compounding geometric component; compensation regime based on whether R < 1, |R−1| ≤ 0.05, or R > 1.

02 — RQ3

Remedy Effectiveness on Real-World Datasets

G-mean averaged across three classifiers and 30 CV runs per (dataset, remedy). Datasets grouped by FIIS diagnosis into three hypothesis groups. Statistical comparison: Friedman test + Nemenyi post-hoc (α = 0.05), pairwise Wilcoxon with Bonferroni correction (αB = 0.05/15).

Case Study: Vertebral Column Diagnosis

End-to-end application of the FIIS diagnostic pipeline to the UCI Vertebral Column dataset (n = 310, IR = 2.1): Normal spine (minority, n1 = 100) vs Abnormal (majority, n0 = 210), six biomechanical features. The FIIS diagnosis is stable across all 30 CV folds.
